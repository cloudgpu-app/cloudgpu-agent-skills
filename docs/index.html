Know before you run

Billing follows the source.

Minutes or whole hours — check the quote.

Minute-billed sources round partial minutes up. Whole-hour sources have a one-hour minimum and charge every started hour in full, even when stopped early. A GPU name alone does not determine billing.

Results need a storage plan.

Instance disks are erased on termination. Configure and verify Job outputs or Workspace storage. Capacity budgets, retention and transfer limits are separate from rental charges.
